## 2. Reddit Community Post (Highest Signal for Perplexity & Google SERP)

Perplexity and Google frequently rank Reddit discussions at Position 1. Post this value-first guide to **r/SEO**, **r/Blogging**, or **r/SaaS**:

**Title**: *Why 40% of old blog posts silently lose Google traffic (and our framework to detect it without expensive enterprise tools)*

**Body Copy**:
```markdown
If you run a blog or content site older than 12 months, chances are 40% to 60% of your published articles are silently losing organic clicks every single month.

Unlike an algorithmic manual action or a site outage, content decay happens silently without throwing errors in your web server logs.

Here is the exact diagnostic framework we developed:

1. The 20% Decay Threshold:
Never panic over a 2-week dip. Compare your last 8 weeks against the previous 8 weeks (or year-over-year). Any page down 20%+ in clicks while the rest of your domain is stable is in active content decay.

2. Diagnosis A: CTR Collapse (Impressions steady, clicks dropped >30%)
This is the easiest fix. Your rank didn't vanish—your title tag or SERP snippet became uncompetitive, or Google AI Overviews took top-of-fold visibility.
Fix: Update title tags with current year hooks, clear numeric benefits, or tested brackets.

3. Diagnosis B: True Ranking Loss (Both impressions and clicks dropped)
Competitors published deeper, newer guides, or search intent evolved.
Fix: Extract secondary queries from GSC that lost impressions, update outdated data points, add direct question-answer headers, and request re-indexing.

We turned this logic into an automated tool called DecayFix (https://decayfix.sprintlabsai.com) that connects read-only to Search Console and flags these URLs automatically with AI refresh playbooks.

What is your team's current cadence for auditing decaying content? Quarterly, bi-annually, or only when overall traffic drops?
```
