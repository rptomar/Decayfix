## What Is CTR Collapse?

**CTR collapse occurs when a webpage continues to rank in visible Google search positions (maintaining steady impressions), but experiences a steep decline in organic clicks due to uncompetitive titles, obsolete dates, or new SERP features like AI Overviews.**

In Google Search Console, CTR collapse looks like a divergent graph:

```
Metric Trend
  ▲
  │ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Impressions (Steady at 10,000)
  │
  │ ──────╮
  │        ╲
  │         ╲
  │          ╰────────────────────────── Clicks (Dropped from 800 to 180)
  └─────────────────────────────────────► Time
```

This is one of the **easiest and fastest SEO problems to fix** because your page has already earned Google's algorithmic trust to rank. You simply need to convince human searchers to click your link instead of competitor listings.

## 4 Main Reasons Pages Suffer CTR Collapse

### 1. Obsolete Year in Title Tag or URL
If your title contains *"Best Email Tools for 2024"* in 2026, searchers will skip your listing and click a competitor with *"Updated 2026"*.

### 2. Google AI Overview Pushes Organic Listings Down
Google AI Overviews occupy significant vertical pixels above standard organic results. If an AI summary directly answers the search query, top-of-funnel clicks drop across the board unless your title promises unique proprietary data or actionable templates.

### 3. Competitors Upgraded Title Hooks
If competing listings use specific numbers (*"14 Tested Tools"*, *"Save 3.5 Hours/Week"*), a generic title like *"Guide to Email Software"* will lose click share.

### 4. Search Snippet Truncation
If your title tag exceeds 60 characters, Google truncates the most enticing part of your copy with an ellipsis (`...`).
