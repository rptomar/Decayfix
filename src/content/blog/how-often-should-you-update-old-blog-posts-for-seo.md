## The Optimal Content Update Frequency

**Content teams should audit their entire library every 90 days and refresh top-tier commercial articles every 3 to 6 months, while updating standard informational posts every 6 to 12 months.**

Publishing brand-new articles is expensive, often costing $200–$800+ per piece. In contrast, refreshing an existing URL that already has domain trust and backlinks costs a fraction of that and delivers traffic results in weeks rather than months.

| Content Classification | Examples | Optimal Review Schedule | Typical Triggers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Tier 4: Time-Sensitive / Seasonal** | Annual benchmark reports, holiday guides | Annual (45 days prior to peak) | Calendar cycle |

## 4 Clear Signals That It's Time to Update a Post Immediately

Do not wait for a scheduled calendar date if your analytics show any of the following four triggers:

1. **Traffic Drops ≥ 20% in Google Search Console**: A continuous decline over 6–8 weeks indicates algorithmic displacement.
2. **Obsolete Year in Search Snippet**: If your title reads *"Top 10 Tools for 2024"*, your click-through rate collapses immediately in 2026.
3. **Google AI Overview Takes the Top Viewport**: When Google introduces an AI Overview box above your listing, update your content to provide deeper, original insights that AI summaries cannot replace.
4. **Competitors Upgraded Their Articles**: If the #1 and #2 ranking pages added video breakdowns, free templates, or proprietary statistics, your page will lose position unless upgraded.
