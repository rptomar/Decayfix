## 2. Google Search Console (Native Date Comparison)
**Best For**: Comprehensive manual analysis for users comfortable with spreadsheet exports.

* **How it works**: Open **Performance > Search Results > Date Filter > Compare last 3 months to previous period**.
* **What you get**: Unfiltered first-party impression, click, and ranking data for every URL on your domain.
* **Cost**: 100% Free.

---

## 3. Google Looker Studio (Free GSC Template)
**Best For**: Visualizing domain-wide traffic trends on interactive dashboards.

* **How it works**: Connect your Search Console data source to a free Looker Studio report template.
* **What you get**: Interactive visual charts plotting weekly clicks, impressions, and CTR by landing page.
* **Cost**: 100% Free.

---

## 4. Google Analytics 4 (Landing Page Comparison)
**Best For**: Tracking downstream conversions and bounce rate changes on declining articles.

* **How it works**: Navigate to **Reports > Engagement > Pages and screens**, and enable date comparison.
* **What you get**: Understand whether declining traffic also resulted in fewer leads, signups, or affiliate purchases.
* **Cost**: 100% Free.

---

## 5. Google Trends
**Best For**: Verifying whether an article's traffic drop is caused by declining industry interest (seasonality).

* **How it works**: Enter your primary topic or keyword into [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com).
* **What you get**: 5-year macro search demand curves to distinguish market-wide drops from website-specific decay.
* **Cost**: 100% Free.
