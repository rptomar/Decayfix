While large SEO suites focus on tracking keywords you manually define, content decay tools inspect your entire URL library to catch silent traffic loss across thousands of unexpected long-tail queries.

## Detailed Tool Reviews

### 1. DecayFix (by SprintLabs.ai)
**The Dedicated Content Decay Detection & AI Playbook Engine**

DecayFix connects directly to Google Search Console via official read-only OAuth and immediately calculates traffic variations across a full 16-month historical baseline. It isolates URLs with a 20%+ decline in clicks or impressions, classifies the drop (CTR collapse vs. ranking loss), and provides specific 2-to-4 sentence AI refresh directives.

* **Pros**: 
  * Connects in 10 seconds with zero tracking code or DNS setup.
  * Dedicated 20% decay threshold logic that sorts by absolute lost clicks.
  * Practical AI action playbooks for every decaying URL.
  * High affordability (₹999/mo / ~$12 USD) with a 7-day money-back guarantee.
* **Cons**: Focuses specifically on Search Console decay diagnostics and playbooks; does not include backlink databases or rank tracking for non-connected competitor domains.

### 2. Google Search Console (Native)
**The Raw Source of Truth**

Google Search Console is Google's official webmaster tool. By navigating to **Performance > Search Results > Date Compare**, you can identify URLs with declining performance.

* **Pros**: 100% free, direct first-party data directly from Google without third-party sampling.
* **Cons**: No automated alerts for decaying pages, requires manual exports to spreadsheets, and provides zero editorial advice on how to fix pages.

### 3. Ahrefs
**The Enterprise SEO Powerhouse**

Ahrefs is a premier all-in-one SEO platform. Its *Top Pages* and *Site Explorer* modules allow users to compare organic traffic over time.

* **Pros**: Unmatched backlink intelligence, competitor keyword analysis, and extensive SERP history.
* **Cons**: High price point starting at $99/mo (with restrictive credit limits on entry tiers); requires manual investigation to identify specific decayed URLs.

### 4. Clearscope
**The Gold Standard for Content Quality Monitoring**

Clearscope offers a Content Inventory feature that tracks the semantic content grade of your published articles over time as search intent evolves.

* **Pros**: Exceptional NLP recommendations and real-time grading for content freshness.
* **Cons**: Premium pricing ($199–$399/month), best suited for large enterprise editorial teams.
