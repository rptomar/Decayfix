## When to Use a Traditional Rank Tracker

Traditional rank trackers (like Wincher, AccuRanker, or Nightwatch) are ideal when:
* You are launching a new product and want to track daily rank shifts on 20 specific head terms.
* You need to track non-owned competitor website rankings in international SERPs.
* You require daily rank updates for client executive reporting.
